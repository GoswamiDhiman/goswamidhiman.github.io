About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at George Mason University, advised by Zhicong Lu and Sanchari Das. My dissertation develops privacy-preserving, user-centered NLP methods for detecting transliterated offensive content on social media, with a focus on South Asian multilingual contexts such as Bangla and Hindi. My work brings together Natural Language Processing, Human-Computer Interaction, and Privacy, and has appeared at venues including NAACL, EMNLP, EACL, NDSS, AsiaCCS, WiSec, and PETs.

Before my Ph.D., I was a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University, Bangladesh (2017–2020).

Research Interests: Natural Language Processing, Human-Computer Interaction, Usable Privacy & Security
